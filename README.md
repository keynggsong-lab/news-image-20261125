## Stories covered

1. **Strait of Hormuz:** two oil tankers struck naval mines, exploded and caught fire while transiting the strait (IRGC statement, 25 Nov).
2. **Saudi Arabia:** Yemen's Houthis said they attacked the Ras Tanura oil refinery, a day after a Houthi strike on a Riyadh tower injured 11 people.
3. **Huawei convicted:** a US federal jury in Brooklyn found Huawei guilty of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud (acquitted on racketeering).
4. **Shenzhou-24:** China launched Shenzhou-24 and docked with Tiangong the same day, carrying Muhammad Zeeshan Ali — the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong.
5. **Pacific:** Australia and Solomon Islands signed a treaty in Canberra precluding any foreign military or security base on Solomon Islands.
6. **Markets:** Asian stocks tumbled on AI doubts — South Korea's KOSPI fell 6.2%, Nikkei 2.8% — while the Dow closed above 50,000 for the first time.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Copies `entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, 782,045 bytes) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`) and verifies the copy size matches. Python 3.13, standard library only, no network.
